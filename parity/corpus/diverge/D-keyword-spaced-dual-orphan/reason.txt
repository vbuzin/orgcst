D-keyword-spaced-dual-orphan
An orphaned #+CAPTION[short cap]: line is a keyword here and a paragraph in Org.
Deliberate: Org's keyword regexp stops at the space in the dual, so the line
falls back to a paragraph only when it does not attach. Here every keyword
line has one shape, attached or not, and an orphaned caption is a plain
keyword exactly as #+CAPTION: long is in Org. Caption meaning comes only from
attachment (AFFILIATED_KEYWORDS), so nothing reads it as a caption. The
difference in Emacs is export: Org prints the line as paragraph text.
